Earliest entry by night: Sun & Tue hold from the 5 PM reopen — get in early. Mon & Thu stop out at the reopen (deep early dip), so wait for ~8 PM. Wed rarely holds early — 11 PM continuation or skip. Monday is the prime night (56% carry 800t+, often into the afternoon). Early/overnight holds need ~400t of room; the 11 PM continuation takes less heat (~250t). ~5–10 samples/night — trust the ranking over the exact %.
Method: Ignore the predicted direction — wait for price to clear ~50t off the session open, enter in that direction, target the listed move, stop at the listed level. ENTER = typical move-start once it declares. EXIT/PEAK = where the move tops (Asia/London) or the morning fade (NY). STOP = the post-entry heat that ~80% of continuing moves stayed within. x/y = how many recent weeks the confirmation actually continued (+100t) — your false-breakout gauge.
Position size (auto 2/3): Each session is tagged 3 micros when its stop is ≤180t and 2 micros when wider — so the wide-stop sessions (NY Mon/Thu, London Tue, Asia Tue) don't balloon your risk. This holds dollar risk roughly even (~$200–500/trade on MGC) instead of letting it swing 4×. Uncheck Auto-size to set a flat contract count instead.
Dollars: Each tick (0.10) = $1.00 per MGC contract (GC is 10×). STOP $ = risk per trade at the shown size; TARGET $ = projected gain if it reaches the mean move (a projection, not a guarantee — peaks vary).
Caveats: Stops/continuation from ~8 sessions per weekday (noisy — trust the pattern, not the exact tick). NY Thursday stays volatile (wide stop). London Tuesday confirmations fake out as often as they work. Rolling window shifts each refresh. Not trading advice.
